# A3GK brand guide (paste-ready for marketing tools such as Holo)

## One-line description
A3GK is a goalkeeper-focused brand from A3 Goalkeeping. The first product is a padded base layer worn underneath goalkeeper kit: front-only honeycomb padding where keepers take impact, smooth fabric everywhere else.

## Logos (white versions for dark backgrounds)
- Mark: `public/brand/a3-mark-white.png` (the A with the swoosh and the small 3).
- Lockup: `public/brand/a3-lockup-white.png` ("A3 Sports Solutions").
- Originals are black on white; use the black originals on light backgrounds. Do not redraw, recolour, stretch or add effects.
- Keep clear space around the mark of at least half the height of the "3".

## Colours
| Role | Value |
|---|---|
| Background | near-black, `#080A0E` to `#121418` (charcoal) |
| Text | white, with white at 60-80% opacity for secondary text |
| Accent (buttons, highlights, price badge) | lime `#D6FF4A` (RGB 214, 255, 74). Use sparingly. |
| Light surface (rare) | bone `#F4F3EF` |

Photography and renders are dark and moody: floodlit night pitch, wet grass, mist, black garments with subtle blue-grey rim light.

## Fonts
- Headlines: **Barlow Condensed**, bold or extra-bold, UPPERCASE, tight line height. Some headlines use an outlined first word.
- Body: **Inter**.

## Voice
Focused, practical, plain. Short sentences. Talk like a coach: say what it is, where the padding is, what it costs, who it is for. No hype, no slang, no emojis in product copy, no exclamation marks.

Examples of the tone: "Goalkeeper kit starts underneath." / "Padded where it counts." / "Pick a piece. Or pair it."

## Facts you may state
- Fabric: **100% premium polyester**. Padding: **EVA foam padding that moves with your body**.
- Fit: **designed to fit tight to your body**.
- Padding is on the **front only**. The back is plain fabric.
- Long-sleeve top: chest, ribs, upper arms, elbows and forearms. Leggings: joined hip-and-thigh panel and a knee panel. Short-sleeve top: chest, ribs, a panel on each short sleeve (no elbow panels). Shorts: joined hip-and-thigh panel (no knee panels).
- Sold individually and in any combination: **$50 CAD per piece, $80 CAD for one top plus one bottom** (save $20).
- Designed to sit under a long-sleeve jersey and long pants. It can be worn under short sleeves and shorts, but that is not recommended because it can reduce durability.
- Images of the products and people are computer-made renderings based on the real garments, not photographs. Say so when it matters.

## Never say (not verified)
- Padding thickness, density, weight or any size or measurement figure (fabric and padding material are confirmed above; nothing else about them is).
- Performance claims such as "moisture-wicking", "breathable", "anti-odour", "compression level" or "stretch" amounts unless confirmed.
- That it prevents, reduces or protects against injury, or is "impact-tested", "certified" or "approved".
- Delivery times, shipping costs, return terms, stock levels, "limited" or "selling fast", reviews, or numbers of customers.
- Any other brand's name, logo or player.
- "SOC 2", "compliant", "award-winning" or similar claims without proof.

## Imagery rules
- Front views only. **Never show padding on the back.**
- No faces: crop at the neck. No generic stock-looking models.
- Under a jersey, the jersey is smooth; padding shows only where the base layer is exposed.
- Panel shapes must match the real product exactly.

## Calls to action
"Build your kit" / "Shop the base layer" / "Join the club". Club = the A3 Goalkeepers Club email list (early notice of new drops; early access where offered).

## Email and consent rules (Canada)
Only email people who opted in. Every marketing email needs the business name, a mailing address and a working unsubscribe link.
